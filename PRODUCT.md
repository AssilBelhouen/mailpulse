# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Users
Invited users manually sending cold emails through Gmail.
## Product Purpose
Create a fresh opaque tracking URL for each intended email and review raw image requests.
## Operating Context
Users insert a remote image in Gmail themselves. Gmail URL insertion has passed a controlled feasibility test; proxies, composition and caching make signals unreliable.
## Capabilities and Constraints
The accepted specification is docs/plan.md. Login, recipient creation, a paginated table, copy controls, refresh and confirmed deletion are the entire V0 interface. No sending, integration, automation, charts, notifications or confirmed-open claims.
## Product Principles
Keep the workflow small. Protect each owner's data. Make uncertainty visible. Never load a tracking image in the dashboard.
